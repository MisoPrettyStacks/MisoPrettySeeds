Questions, answered

How do I pay for my seeds?

Right in our DM conversation! After you message me on X, I'll send you my payment link — Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal, your choice. Quick, simple, no accounts to create.

When will my seeds ship?

Orders go out within a few days of payment. I'll confirm in the DM the moment your pretty packets are on their way.

What if my seeds don't sprout?

Every batch is germination-tested before it goes in a packet — but if something goes wrong, just DM me and we'll make it right. I want you to have flowers, not frustrations.

Can I request a variety that's sold out?

Yes! DM me and I'll put you first in line for the next batch. Small-batch means limited, but it also means I restock with care.
